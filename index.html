Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and chiropractic care.

What should I expect during my first visit?

Your first visit begins with a comprehensive consultation where we'll discuss your health history, current symptoms, and treatment goals. We'll perform a detailed physical examination and may take X-rays or other diagnostic images if needed. This allows us to understand your condition fully and create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs. The entire first visit typically takes 45-60 minutes, and you'll leave with a clear understanding of your condition and the recommended treatment approach.

How many sessions will I need?

The number of sessions depends on your specific condition, severity, and how your body responds to treatment. Acute conditions might require 4-8 visits over a few weeks, while chronic conditions may require ongoing care over several months. During your initial consultation, we'll provide a recommended treatment plan with an estimated timeline. We believe in transparency and will regularly reassess your progress to adjust the plan as needed. Our goal is always to achieve your health objectives as efficiently as possible.

Does insurance cover chiropractic care?

Many insurance plans do cover chiropractic care. We accept most major insurance providers and are happy to verify your coverage before your first appointment. We'll work with your insurance company to maximize your benefits and minimize out-of-pocket costs. For patients without insurance, we offer flexible payment plans and competitive pricing. Contact us at litchiropractic1@gmail.com or call our office to discuss your specific insurance situation.

Is chiropractic care safe?

Yes, chiropractic care is safe when performed by licensed professionals. Our chiropractors are fully licensed and use evidence-based techniques with a proven safety record. During your consultation, we'll discuss your complete medical history to identify any contraindications. We use the latest technology and follow strict safety protocols. Serious complications are rare, and most patients experience only mild, temporary soreness after treatment, similar to exercise. We're committed to your safety and will always discuss any risks and benefits before proceeding with treatment.

Can I book an appointment online?

Absolutely! We offer convenient online booking through our patient portal at litchiropractic.janeapp.com. You can view available appointment times and schedule at your convenience, even outside of office hours. If you prefer to book by phone or email, you can reach us at litchiropractic1@gmail.com. Our flexible scheduling includes early morning, evening, and weekend appointments to accommodate your busy lifestyle. We also offer new patient consultations to discuss your needs before scheduling your first treatment.
